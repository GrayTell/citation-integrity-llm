STRICT CITATION PROMPT — GrayTell Labs Citation Integrity Study
===============================================================

Used to evaluate citation accuracy in DeepSeek, Claude, and ChatGPT.
Date of use: September 2026
Study: Citation Integrity in Frontier Language Models
Author: Anubhav Sapkota (GrayTell Labs)

---------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT TEXT (copy everything between the dashed lines)
---------------------------------------------------------------

Answer the following research question and provide 4-5 academic citations.

You MUST include a separate "References" section.
Each reference must follow exactly:

Authors. (Year). Title. Venue. DOI/URL (if available).

Do not just describe studies - list the actual papers.

Question: [PASTE QUESTION HERE]

---------------------------------------------------------------
END OF PROMPT
---------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS USED
--------------

Q3 (Biology / Medicine):
What is the current evidence on CRISPR-based therapies for sickle cell disease?

Q4 (Biology / Medicine):
How does the gut microbiome influence neurodegenerative diseases such as
Parkinson's and Alzheimer's?

Q5 (Physics):
What is the current status of research on high-temperature superconductivity
in hydrogen-rich materials?

NOTE: Q1 and Q2 (AI/ML questions on Constitutional AI and RAG limitations)
were pilot questions. Models did not provide formal citations for these
even when asked, so they were excluded from the citation analysis.

---------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFICATION PROTOCOL
---------------------------------------------------------------

For each citation the model produces, verify using:

  - Google Scholar (exact title search)
  - PubMed (for biomedical papers)
  - DOI resolution (https://doi.org/[DOI])
  - Publisher website (NEJM, Nature, Science, etc.)

Check each field:
  [ ] Does the paper exist?
  [ ] Are the authors correct?
  [ ] Is the year correct?
  [ ] Is the title correct?
  [ ] Is the venue (journal/conference) correct?
  [ ] Does the DOI resolve to the correct paper?

Label each citation as:

  - Real & Accurate       - all fields correct
  - Real but Distorted    - paper exists, but at least one field is wrong
  - Fully Fabricated      - no evidence the paper exists
